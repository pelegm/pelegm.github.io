About

I am currently a Nicholas Kurti Junior Research Fellow at Brasenose College and a Marie Skłodowska–Curie Postdoctoral Fellow in the Combinatorics group at the University of Oxford, supervised by Peter Keevash. My research interests are in extremal and probabilistic combinatorics, as well as in discrete probability. Previously, I served as a Postdoctoral Associate at Carnegie Mellon University. I completed my PhD at Tel Aviv University under the joint supervision of Michael Krivelevich and Asaf Nachmias. Prior to that, I earned my MSc at Tel Aviv University, also under the supervision of Michael Krivelevich. My Erdős number is 2.
